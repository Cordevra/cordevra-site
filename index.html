Features

Built for how accounting firms actually work

Every piece of your practice, connected — so nothing slips between tools.

Recurring workflows

Cycle-based workflows for monthly closes, payroll, and tax season that roll forward on their own.

Clients & leads

One record per client with contacts, services, notes, and history. Leads live right alongside them.

Client portal

A clean dashboard where clients upload documents, answer questions, and see what's needed from them.

Custom forms

Build your own onboarding and intake forms — no extra subscription, answers land on the client record.

Document storage

Native, organized client folders built in — no syncing to a separate drive.

Billing & payments

Invoice clients and get paid online with embedded payments, tied to the work you delivered.

QuickBooks Online review

Clients review uncategorized transactions and approve A/P bills right from their portal.

Calendar & booking

A built-in calendar with booking links for discovery calls, reviews, and client meetings.

Playbook

Document your firm's procedures and SOPs once, so every team member does the work the same way.

How it works

Work that runs in cycles — not chaos

Accounting is recurring by nature. cordevra is built around that rhythm, so every month starts organized.

1
OnboardSend a custom intake form. The client, documents, and services are set up automatically.
2
Run the cycleRecurring workflows create the right tasks, deadlines, and client requests every period.
3
CollaborateClients answer, upload, and approve in their portal — no more chasing over email.
4
Bill & repeatInvoice, get paid, and watch the next cycle roll forward on its own.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is cordevra for?

Bookkeeping, accounting, and tax firms — from solo practitioners to growing teams — that want clients, work, and billing in one place.

When does it launch?

We're building now. Waitlist members get first access and launch updates before anyone else.

How much will it cost?

Prices will be announced before launch. Waitlist members get founding-member pricing, locked for life. See the plans above.

How is my team billed?

We'll share how team members and seats are priced with waitlist members before launch.

Does it work with QuickBooks Online?

Yes — QuickBooks Online is a core part of the plan, starting with client transaction review and A/P bill approvals in the portal.

Can I switch from my current practice management tool?

Yes. We're planning a simple path to bring your clients, contacts, and recurring work over, and early-access firms will get help with the move.

How will my data be protected?

Security is being built in from the start, and we'll publish our security practices before launch. The live demo uses sample data only, so please don't enter real client information there.

Is cordevra for business owners too?

Yes. Lens, Scope, and Network are also designed for business owners who use QuickBooks Online. Join the waitlist in the business owner section above.
