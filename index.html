FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is cordevra for?

Bookkeeping, accounting and tax firms, from solo practitioners to growing teams, that want clients, work and billing in one place.

How much does it cost?

Solo is $29/month billed yearly ($39 month-to-month) for 1 user and up to 50 clients. Practice is $49 per user/month billed yearly ($59 month-to-month). Practice Plus is $69 per user/month billed yearly ($85 month-to-month) and adds AI, deeper reports, custom roles, a white-label portal and 250 text/WhatsApp messages per user each month.

What do founding firms get?

The first 50 firms get 30% off for their first 12 months and free migration of their clients, contacts and recurring work. Join the waitlist to claim a spot.

What does it cost to take client payments?

Card payments cost Stripe's fee plus 0.5%. ACH bank payments cost 1%, capped at $10 per payment.

Do my clients pay for portal access?

No. Client portal users are free and unlimited on every plan.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 14 days, and no card is needed to start.

When does it launch?

We're building now. Waitlist members get first access and launch updates before anyone else.

Does it work with QuickBooks Online?

Yes. QuickBooks Online is included in every plan, starting with client transaction review and bill approvals in the portal. When Cordevra Advisory launches, it costs $6 per connected client per month (first 3 clients free) and comes with ready-made dashboards and report packs; building your own dashboards comes later.

Can I switch from my current practice management tool?

Yes. We'll bring your clients, contacts and recurring work over, and the migration is free for founding firms.

How will my data be protected?

Security is built in from the start: two-step sign-in, encryption of sensitive fields, separate data for every firm and a full audit log. The live demo uses sample data only, so please don't enter real client information there.

Is cordevra for business owners too?

Yes. A plan for business owners on QuickBooks Online is coming later at $39/month, with Cordevra Advisory (Lens + Scope), and Cordevra Network will help them hire professionals, free at launch. Join the waitlist in the business owner section above.
