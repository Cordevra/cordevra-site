Features

Built for how accounting firms actually work

Every piece of your practice, connected — so nothing slips between tools.

Recurring workflows

Cycle-based workflows for monthly closes, payroll, and tax season that roll forward on their own.

Clients & leads

One record per client with contacts, services, notes, and history. Leads live right alongside them.

Client portal

A clean dashboard where clients upload documents, answer questions, and see what's needed from them.

Custom forms

Build your own onboarding and intake forms — no extra subscription, answers land on the client record.

Document storage

Native, organized client folders built in — no syncing to a separate drive.

Billing & payments

Invoice clients and get paid online with embedded payments, tied to the work you delivered.

QuickBooks Online review

Clients review uncategorized transactions and approve A/P bills right from their portal.

Calendar & booking

A built-in calendar with booking links for discovery calls, reviews, and client meetings.

Knowledge base & SOPs

Document your firm's procedures once, so every team member does the work the same way.

How it works

Work that runs in cycles — not chaos

Accounting is recurring by nature. cordevra is built around that rhythm, so every month starts organized.

1
OnboardSend a custom intake form. The client, documents, and services are set up automatically.
2
Run the cycleRecurring workflows create the right tasks, deadlines, and client requests every period.
3
CollaborateClients answer, upload, and approve in their portal — no more chasing over email.
4
Bill & repeatInvoice, get paid, and watch the next cycle roll forward on its own.
