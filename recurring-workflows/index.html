Recurring workflows

Recurring workflows for bookkeeping firms: month-end that runs itself

Accounting work repeats. Cordevra is built around that rhythm: monthly closes, payroll and tax season roll forward on their own, with the right tasks, deadlines and client requests every period.

The problem: rebuilding the same month, every month

A bookkeeping practice runs on repetition. Every client has a monthly close. Many have payroll every pay period, quarterly filings, and year-end work that arrives all at once. In a spreadsheet or a general to-do app, someone has to recreate that work every period: copy last month's checklist, update the dates, remember which clients need extra steps, and send the document requests by hand.

When that copying slips, the work does not disappear; it just becomes invisible until a client asks why their books are behind. The bigger the firm, the harder it gets to answer a simple question: are we on track this month?

How recurring workflows work in Cordevra

In Cordevra you define a workflow once, as a template, and assign it to the clients it applies to. A typical monthly bookkeeping cycle looks like this:

  1. Request documents: statements and reports go out as requests in the client's portal.
  2. Reconcile the accounts once the documents arrive.
  3. Review the work before it reaches the client.
  4. Client questions: open items go out through QuickBooks review.
  5. Close the period.
  6. Invoice for the work you delivered.

When a period ends, the next one is created on its own with the right tasks, deadlines and client requests. September closes, October is in progress, November is already scheduled to start on the first of the month.

See every client's progress at once

The Work Management list shows every recurring project with its status, progress, deadlines and risk alerts. Switch to the board view to drag projects through the week: overdue, this week, next week and later. Anything blocked by a client shows up on the Waiting on client board, so you can tell the difference between work your team has not started and work that is stuck on a missing bank statement.

Built for teams

On the Practice plan, workflows come with team roles, assignments and reviews, so the person who prepares the close is not the person who signs it off. Your firm's procedures can live in the Playbook for SOPs, next to the work they describe. Practice Plus adds capacity and profit reports, and Ask Cordevra AI, which answers plain-language questions over your own firm's work, such as "who is still waiting on documents for the October close?"

Not just month-end

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of work can be recurring?

Monthly closes, payroll, tax season and any other cycle your firm repeats. Each period gets the right tasks, deadlines and client requests automatically.

Do client document requests repeat too?

Yes. Requests are part of the workflow, so each period's requests appear in the client portal without being set up again.

Can I see which clients are behind?

Yes. Work Management shows status, progress, deadlines and risk alerts for every project, and the board view groups them by overdue, this week, next week and later.

Does it support review before work goes to the client?

Yes. The Practice plan includes team roles, assignments and reviews.
