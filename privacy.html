Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Cordevra collects through cordevra.com and how we use it.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, the form sends us your email address and any details you choose to include, such as firm size, role, and the tools you use today. The waitlist is stored in Google Forms, part of our Google Workspace account. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. We don't use tracking cookies or advertising pixels on this site.

How we use it

We use this information only to manage the waitlist, send launch updates, and understand who Cordevra should serve first. We don't sell or rent your information.

The live demo

The demo at demo.cordevra.com runs in your browser with sample data. Please don't enter real client information there.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove you from the waitlist or delete your information at any time by emailing us.

Questions? Email support@cordevra.com.
